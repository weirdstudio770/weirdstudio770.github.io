Privacy-First & Offline

Privacy Policy for Let Focus

Effective Date: September 2026

🛡️ Summary: Let Focus does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any of your personal information. The app functions 100% offline without accounts, advertisements, or analytics tracking.

1. Introduction

At Let Focus, we respect your right to privacy and mindful productivity. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning user data, device permissions, and local storage.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Because Let Focus is built on an offline-first architecture:

  • No Account Registration: You do not need to log in or provide email addresses, names, or phone numbers.
  • No Analytics or Trackers: We do not integrate tracking SDKs, crash report collectors, or behavioral monitoring tools.
  • No Network Transmission: The release version of Let Focus explicitly removes network permissions (android.permission.INTERNET). None of your tasks, habits, notes, or timer logs can ever leave your device through the app.

3. Local Storage and Data Ownership

All content created in Let Focus resides exclusively on your local device storage:

  • Data Storage: Pomodoro logs, tasks, habits, and rhythm configurations are stored in an isolated local database on your device.
  • Backup & Export: You can export your data anytime as a standard JSON file to back up or transfer between devices.
  • Data Deletion: You can wipe all stored data at any time via Settings > Your data > Erase all data, or by clearing app storage / uninstalling the app.

4. Device Permissions

Let Focus requests only minimal permissions necessary to deliver reliable timer operations and notifications:

  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used on Android 13+ to notify you when focus blocks or breaks finish, and for habit reminders you configure.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE): Displays a live countdown notification while a session is active and ensures the timer process is not killed by the OS.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Ensures that interval completion alarms sound accurately to the exact second, even when your phone is in deep sleep / standby.
  • Do Not Disturb Policy (ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY): Optional. Allows Let Focus to silence incoming notifications during a focus block and restore them during breaks.
  • Battery Optimization Exemption (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS): Optional. Allows you to exempt the timer from aggressive system battery management so background sessions aren't terminated.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Delivers haptic feedback and vibrations upon interval completion.
  • Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK): Keeps the screen gently illuminated while viewing the clock in Zen Mode.

5. Third-Party Services

Let Focus does not use any third-party services, APIs, or ad networks.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
