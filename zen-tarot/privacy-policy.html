Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application Zen Tarot ("Application") for mobile devices. The Application provides daily tarot card readings, timeline readings, and an educational encyclopedia fully offline.

1. Information We Collect and Use

The Application is designed to function primarily offline. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or exact physical location) to our own servers.

However, to support app maintenance, monitor performance, and deliver advertisements, the Application utilizes integrated third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs). These third-party services may automatically collect certain non-sensitive data, specifically Device or Other IDs (such as Google Advertising ID, Android ID, or unique identifiers for analytics), which are transmitted and processed securely in transit using encryption (HTTPS).

2. Third-Party Services

To support continuous development and monitor technical stability, we integrate the following third-party SDKs:

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the Application to fund continuous development. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), usage data, and cookies to serve, personalize, or measure the effectiveness of advertisements. For more information, please visit: Google Advertising Policies.

Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to monitor application stability, detect bugs, and resolve performance issues. When a crash occurs, this service automatically collects anonymous crash logs, device models, operating system versions, and system state information. This data helps us improve the app and does not contain personal information. For more details, please visit: Firebase Privacy and Security.

Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the Application and to measure general usage patterns (such as screen views and session duration). This service collects anonymous interaction metrics and device identifiers to generate aggregated statistical reports. This helps us optimize user experience. For more information, please visit: Google Analytics Partner Sites Policy.

3. Offline Storage and Data Security

All your tarot reading history, individual preferences, and selected application settings are stored strictly locally on your mobile device. They are not uploaded to any remote server managed by us. Please note that if you delete the Application or clear its data via device settings, all locally stored progress will be permanently erased.

4. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided third-party services within our app with personal information, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
