Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application Zen Tarot ("Application"), created to provide intuitive Tarot divination, daily mindfulness guidance, and a comprehensive card encyclopedia.

Zen Tarot is designed as an offline-first, privacy-respecting sanctuary. We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide your personal identity to enjoy any of our Tarot reading features.

1. Information We Collect and Storage Architecture

The Application collects minimal data necessary to function, maintain stability, and serve compliant advertisements:

  • Local User Data (100% On-Device): All your Tarot reading journals, personal reflection notes, card draw history, and app preferences (theme, language, sound, and notifications) are saved exclusively locally on your device using encrypted key-value storage. We do not transmit or store your personal Tarot readings on any remote server.
  • Device and Usage Information (Via Third-Party SDKs): When your device is connected to the internet, our integrated partners automatically receive standard non-sensitive technical identifiers, including your Google Advertising ID, operating system version, device model, and crash diagnostics. All transmissions use secure HTTPS/TLS encryption.

2. Third-Party Services and Analytics

To fund ongoing development, monitor application reliability, and understand feature usage, the Application integrates industry-standard third-party services:

AdvertisingGoogle AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and app open advertisements. AdMob uses device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID or IDFA) to serve, personalize, and measure ad performance in compliance with platform rules.

Read more: Google Advertising Policies

DiagnosticsFirebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to detect unexpected application crashes and bugs. When an error occurs, it securely transmits crash stack traces, device hardware details, and OS versions so we can release fixes promptly. This data is pseudonymous and contains no personal tarot records.

Read more: Firebase Privacy and Security

AnalyticsFirebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to aggregate anonymous usage statistics (such as popular spreads or reading types) to help us refine user experience. You can opt out of analytics tracking at device level.

Read more: Firebase Analytics Policy

3. European Users: GDPR and Google UMP Consent

Your Rights Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

If you reside within the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you are protected by the General Data Protection Regulation and the ePrivacy Directive.

Consent Management: We have implemented the certified Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. Before personalized ads or tracking identifiers are activated, a standardized GDPR consent dialogue is presented asking for your explicit consent.

How to Revoke or Change Your Consent: You have the absolute right to revisit, change, or revoke your consent preferences at any time. Inside the Zen Tarot application, simply open:

⚙️ Settings → Privacy → Ad Privacy Choices

Tapping this option will immediately reload the Google Consent Dialog, allowing you to update vendor consents or switch between personalized and non-personalized advertising.

4. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and CPRA, California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data. Zen Tarot does not sell your personal information. Third-party advertising partners comply with Google's Restricted Data Processing (RDP) protocols where applicable.

5. Children's Privacy

The Application is intended for general audiences aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided data through our third-party SDKs, please contact us and we will promptly ensure appropriate steps are taken.

6. Offline Data Retention and Deletion

Since all your reading journals and personal notes reside solely on your device's internal storage, you have total control over your data:

  • You can clear all reading records instantly inside the app by going to Settings → Clear Reading History.
  • Uninstalling the Application or clearing its app data in your device's System Settings will permanently erase all local journal entries.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory developments. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
