Privacy Policy for Hill Glide

Effective Date: September 20, 2026 • Last Updated: September 20, 2026

Welcome to Hill Glide. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles, collects, and protects information when you play the game.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Hill Glide is designed as an offline-capable casual arcade game. We do not require account creation, registration, or sign-ins. We do not collect or store personal identifying information such as:

  • Your name, address, or phone number
  • Your email address (unless you voluntarily contact our support team)
  • Your financial, banking, or credit card details
  • Your biometric data or contacts list

All gameplay progression, records, coins, high scores, upgrades, and unlocked skins are stored locally on your device storage.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

Hill Glide is supported by advertisements to provide the game free of charge. We integrate Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.

Third-party SDK providers like Google AdMob may automatically collect certain device and usage information to serve contextual or personalized ads, detect fraud, and report analytics. This information may include:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) on Android devices, device model, operating system version, and system language.
  • Coarse Location: General region or approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • App Performance & Diagnostics: Ad impressions, interactions, load failures, and crash diagnostics.

For more details on how Google processes and protects advertising data, please review Google's official privacy documentation:
How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. GDPR and User Consent (EEA & UK)

For players located in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, Hill Glide integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage consent for personalized and non-personalized advertising under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

You have full control over your consent choices. You can review, modify, or revoke your consent at any time via the in-game settings.

4. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Hill Glide is intended for a general audience (ages 13 and older). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information to third-party services through our game without parental consent, we will take prompt steps to delete such information.

5. Data Retention and Security

Because we do not operate external user account databases, we do not retain your personal data on proprietary servers. All network transmissions initiated by integrated services (such as Google Mobile Ads) use industry-standard HTTPS encryption in transit.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.
