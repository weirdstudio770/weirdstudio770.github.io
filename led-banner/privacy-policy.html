LED Banner Scroller

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application LED Banner ("Application") for mobile devices. The Application provides customizable scrolling text, speed control, color selectors, flashing effects, and blinking animations, allowing you to turn your device into a dynamic LED banner scroller completely offline.

1. Information We Collect and Use

The Application is designed to work completely offline. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect or store any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or physical location) on our own servers.

However, the Application utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, optimize performance, and serve advertisements.

2. Third-Party Services

To support the application and continuously monitor technical performance, we integrate the following third-party SDKs:

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the Application to fund continuous development. AdMob may collect and use personalized or non-personalized data, such as device identifiers (Advertising ID), cookies, and usage data to serve relevant ads. For more information, please visit: Google Advertising Policies.

3. Offline Storage and Data Security

All your banner configurations, history, speed/color preferences, and settings are stored locally on your mobile device. They are not uploaded to any remote server managed by us. If you delete or clear the Application's data, all local banners and preferences will be permanently erased.

4. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.
