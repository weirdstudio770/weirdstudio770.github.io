YouOke Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

YouOke is a karaoke app that helps users discover and play karaoke songs through YouTube video links. This policy explains what information the app handles and how third-party services may process data.

Last updated: May 10, 2026

YouTube Videos and Content Ownership

YouOke uses YouTube video links for karaoke playback. We do not own, host, upload, store, or control the YouTube videos shown in the app. All video content, thumbnails, titles, and related media remain the property of their respective owners, artists, publishers, channels, or rights holders.

Videos are played through YouTube services, and availability may change if a video owner removes, restricts, blocks, or changes a video. If you believe a linked video infringes your rights, please contact YouTube or the content owner through YouTube's official copyright process. You may also contact us and we will review requests to remove links from our app listing data.

Information We Store on Your Device

YouOke does not require account registration. The app may save basic preferences locally on your device so the app can work smoothly, including:

  • Favorite song IDs
  • Saved playlist names and song IDs
  • Selected language preference

This information is stored locally on your device and is not used by us to identify you personally.

Third-Party Services

YouOke uses third-party services that may collect or process data under their own privacy policies:

  • YouTube: used to play karaoke videos and display video thumbnails.
  • Google AdMob: used to show ads, including banner, rewarded, and app-open ads.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: used to help us understand and fix app crashes.
  • OneSignal: used to send push notifications, if notification permission is granted.

These services may process device information, advertising identifiers, crash diagnostics, app interaction data, IP address, or notification identifiers depending on your device settings and permissions.

Internet and API Data

The app downloads song, singer, collection, thumbnail, and YouTube link data from our hosted API. This data is used to display the karaoke library inside the app. We do not use this API to collect your name, email address, phone number, or account profile.

Advertising

YouOke displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers and other signals to provide, limit, measure, or personalize ads depending on your consent, region, and device settings. You can manage ad personalization through your Google or device privacy settings.

Push Notifications

If you allow notifications, YouOke may send app updates or content-related notifications through OneSignal. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Children's Privacy

YouOke is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app links to YouTube videos and displays ads, parents or guardians should supervise use by children and review the privacy practices of YouTube and Google services.

Copyright and Removal Requests

We respect the rights of creators and copyright owners. If you are a rights holder and want a YouTube link, thumbnail, title, or listing removed from YouOke, please email us with enough detail to identify the content. After review, we may remove the related link or listing from our app data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact Us
