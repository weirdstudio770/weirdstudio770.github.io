Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the mobile application Apk Analyzer ("the Application") handles your information. The Application lets you inspect the apps installed on your device and APK files you choose: their versions, size, permissions, components, signing certificate and manifest.

In short: apps and APK files are analysed on your device. We operate no servers, and we do not receive your list of installed apps, your files or your usage history. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, which serves the advertisements that fund the app.

1. Installed Apps

Showing and analysing your apps is the core purpose of the Application. For this it reads the list of installed apps and the information Android holds about each of them, such as name, package name, icon, version, size, permissions, components, signing certificate, install source and install dates.

  • Query all packages (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES) lets the Application see every installed app on Android 11 and above.
  • Access list of installed apps (com.android.permission.GET_INSTALLED_APPS) exists on some devices, such as certain Xiaomi phones. There the Application asks for your permission before it can list your apps.

This information is processed on your device only. We do not collect it, and the Application does not send your list of installed apps to us or use it to choose advertisements.

2. Usage Access (Optional)

If you grant usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) in your system settings, the Application can show when apps were last used and how much storage they take, and can sort and filter by that. The Application works without it, and you can withdraw it at any time in system settings. This information stays on your device.

3. Files

  • When you open an APK file, the Application makes a temporary copy in its private cache folder in order to read it. Only the most recently opened file is kept.
  • When you export an APK or save an app icon, the file is written to the location you choose.
  • The Application does not request access to your whole storage, and none of these files are uploaded.

Sharing an app summary, or opening an app in Google Play or in system settings, happens only when you tap the corresponding action. The app you share to, or Google Play, then handles that information under its own privacy policy.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

The Application keeps the following on your device so it can work between sessions:

  • Your preferences, such as colour scheme and fingerprint format.
  • The package names of apps you recently viewed. Turning off Recently viewed apps in Settings erases this list.
  • The time you first used the Application and the time it last showed a full screen advertisement, used to limit how often advertisements appear.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, these settings may be included in the backup stored with your Google account. Uninstalling the Application, or clearing its data, erases the copy on your device.

5. No Account and No Personal Data Collection by Us

The Application does not require an account. We do not collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location. We operate no servers that receive data from the Application.

6. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

The Application displays advertisements supplied by Google AdMob to fund its development. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and interaction with ads. Google may use this data to show you personalised or non-personalised advertisements depending on the choice you make in the consent screen described below.

For details of how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Advertising Policies.

For advertising, the Application uses internet access, the network state and the Android advertising identifier and advertising services permissions.

7. Your Advertising Choices and Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the Application shows a consent screen provided by Google's User Messaging Platform before serving personalised advertisements. You choose there whether to allow personalised ads.

You can change that choice at any time: open Settings in the Application and select Privacy options. Where the consent screen does not apply in your region, that entry is not shown.

Independently of the Application, you may reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on Android).

8. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Application, please contact us at the address below and we will take appropriate action.

9. Data Security

Because your preferences and the files you open stay on your device, their security depends on your device's own protections, such as a screen lock, an up-to-date operating system, and the storage encryption your device provides.
