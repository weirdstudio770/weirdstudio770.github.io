Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the mobile application Myanmar Unit Converter & Calculator ("Application") handles information. The Application converts traditional Myanmar units (viss, kyat, pae, yway, tin, pyi, acre and others) and international units, and calculates gold, land, crop and timber values. All calculations run on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information on our own servers. The Application does not require an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The numbers you type into calculators are processed on your device and are not sent to us.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To make the Application convenient, the following is saved locally on your device only:

  • Your language, light/dark theme and accent colour.
  • Price and rate fields you entered (for example gold market rate, land rate, crop price, making charge) so you do not have to retype them.
  • Your favourite and recently used unit pairs, and the gold kyat weight setting.

This information is never uploaded by us. Clearing the Application's data or uninstalling it erases it permanently.

3. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only to show advertisements and to know whether the device is online. The calculators work fully offline.
  • Advertising ID: the advertising SDK may use your device's advertising ID, as described below.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Google AdMob

The Application may display banner and app-open advertisements provided by Google AdMob. When ads are shown, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system information, and how you interact with ads, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Google acts as an independent controller of this data. See Google Advertising Policies and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent and choices. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the Application asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. In those regions an "Ad privacy choices" item appears in the app menu so you can review or change your choice at any time. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we hold no personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Data saved on your device (section 2) is removed when you clear the Application's storage or uninstall it. For data collected by Google for advertising, please use Google's tools and controls described above.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through advertising or otherwise, please contact us.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or similar laws) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. We do not hold personal data about you. For data processed by Google for advertising you can exercise your rights with Google, and you can withdraw advertising consent at any time through the "Ad privacy choices" item described above.

8. Accuracy of Calculations

Conversions and prices are provided for general reference. Traditional units can vary by region and trade; please verify important transactions independently.

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page.
